# Proposal

## Why

Repeated lead corrections and executor friction can expose avoidable work, but another persistent observer adds a conversation, continuous input transport and recovery before its usefulness is established. The user selected two short-lived native forks after accepted executor work: executor self-reflection followed by lead self-reflection, with decisions and implementation retained by the real lead.

## What Changes

- Replace the unaccepted persistent-observer design and remove its active candidate code, role and skill selection. Retain rejected patches and private evidence only for recovery; do not deliver both designs.
- Deliver `executor-self-reflection` and `lead-self-reflection` as separate skills with role-specific responsibilities and shared launch/delivery mechanics. Each fork analyzes the available source conversation under an explicit reflection mandate; it cannot resume the source task, edit product/guidance, create tasks or dispatch agents.
- While explicitly enabled, use the consuming board's durable accepted-task closure events. A closed card alone is insufficient: the lead's acceptance must bind the ordinary assignment, source sessions and accepted result. No trigger on first executor report, intermediate correction, cancellation, arbitrary closure or elapsed time.
- Run executor reflection first. Its retained result automatically makes lead reflection eligible; the lead fork receives its own conversation and the executor's reflection, verifies both sides of corrections, and returns concrete recommendations or no finding to the real lead.
- Mark ordinary work, reflection and improvement implementation in owned launch/task metadata. Reflection and improvement descendants cannot automatically trigger further reflection. Keep main work moving and at most one ordinary improvement implementation in flight within existing capacity. The responsible lead may hold an affected release for a concrete incident risk found by reflection, with a reason and checkable release condition; reflection is not a routine release gate.
- Keep beads as task/decision owner. The real lead admits a useful evidenced correction without waiting for votes, creates or updates the appropriate task and applies or delegates it through normal acceptance. Uncertain ideas retain ordinary hypothesis/evaluation routes.
- Preserve installed operation, exact model binding, visible conversations, finite cumulative costs, interruption recovery, public/private boundaries and real consumption. Adopt with quality no worse than the control, no P0/P1 regression and a grounded lead judgment that the lasting benefit justifies complete delivery and maintenance costs. The objective is cheaper future changes and maintenance with timely useful delivery; time/token savings are evidence, not mandatory thresholds or the only possible benefit. Added code, tools, instructions and context burden must earn their ongoing cost.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `delivery-improvement`: accepted-task self-reflection, role-specific synthesis, bounded lead decisions and implementation, nonrecursive lifecycle and verified shared delivery.

### Modified Capabilities

- `orchestration-feedback-loop`: derived reflection evidence, lead-owned task creation and explicit verified-improvement admission without synthetic votes or recursive improvement work.

## Impact

Reuse the delivered role resolver, native fork/control/terminal/receipt owners, shared admission, board feedback, existing beads event journal and installation lifecycle. Extend maintained Rust owners only for remaining gaps; no new provider, task tracker, generic scheduler, transcript warehouse or model observer. Two skills replace the proposed observer skill/role. Reconcile team-lead and board-workflow guidance and existing operational docs.

The retained audit sample and previously prepared comparison inputs do not establish benefit. Candidate authoring and model-free checks can run in parallel with qualification; candidate model use and adoption remain gated by the existing predeclared evaluation in design.md. The changed post-acceptance mechanism requires a relevant outcome horizon and cannot claim to improve a task retrospectively.
