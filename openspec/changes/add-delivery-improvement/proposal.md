# Proposal

## Why

The planning audit found two zero-completion changes whose central fixes already exist in source (`settle-executor-watch-finalization` and `serialize-executor-dispatch-claims`), and an unsupported parallel-test proposal whose cited evidence was unavailable. These are bounded observations of stale planning/evidence, not a measured frequency or proof that another agent will repay its cost. A separately focused observer is a hypothesis for detecting attributable friction and obtaining an applied correction earlier while the user's work continues.

## Evidence and hypothesis

The source anchors are retained in the two correction changes' proposals/designs; the parallel-test evidence gap and return condition belong to `add-evidence-driven-self-improvement-loop/proposal.md`. The audit sample contained four zero-completion changes; it was not a random sample of all development and supplies no time/token savings estimate. Existing feedback triage and a lead's bounded evidence check are the simpler alternatives.

Hypothesis: under the matched real-work panel in `design.md`, the observer produces more independently accepted task outcomes without critical regressions, and reduces total accepted-work time and recorded token cost including its own overhead. The mechanism is earlier attributable diagnosis plus actual assignment and consumption, not merely more findings or cards. Healthy text-heavy work, incorrect lead criticism and unavailable evidence can defeat the hypothesis. Data qualification and a frozen comparison precede dependent implementation; an adverse or inconclusive pilot cannot justify full rollout or additional observer machinery.

## What Changes

- Add an explicitly invoked `delivery-improvement` skill that starts one visible improvement session for the active lead and its work. It observes both lead and executor activity without becoming another lead or spawning workers.
- Analyze all new visible text messages from the user, lead and bound executors, including their addressed exchanges, alongside relevant beads tasks and changes. Exclude reasoning and detailed execution traces from routine input; code diffs, assignment context and check evidence are retrieved for concrete questions. Native collection, deduplication and waiting make no model calls and apply no heuristic importance filter to the text.
- Bound carried context, investigation and cumulative observer cost. Coalesce messages and reduce analysis frequency after no-finding batches while retaining unread input and exposing coverage and budget limits. Compare total time and token effects separately, including cases where observation does not repay its cost.
- Diagnose the cause before prescribing a remedy. Relate assignments, submitted code, the actual lead feedback, corrections and checks to distinguish task-framing defects, executor mistakes and changed requirements. Prefer removing unnecessary work or correcting the owning interface/check over accumulating instructions; evaluate reusable lead and executor practices where supported. Lead, executor, shared and improvement-agent guidance are eligible targets.
- Keep beads as the task and decision owner. Deduplicate by underlying evidence, admit confirmed bounded improvements without waiting for three votes while this mode is active, and retain the ordinary incubator for unverified ideas.
- Require the lead to decide at safe boundaries and dispatch accepted, ready improvements at the next suitable allocation opportunity, or record a concrete deferral and return condition. Permit one ordinary improvement implementation at a time within existing executor capacity.
- Cover improvements in the consuming project and the canonical shared kit, with public-safe kit tasks, existing specification/skill-evaluation requirements, verified installation and explicit evidence of consumption. Activation does not authorize unrelated work or silently change active sessions.
- Complete the loop by checking the relevant user path and recording supported, adverse or inconclusive effects. Preserve the separate experiment controller for hypotheses requiring comparisons rather than imposing its entire workflow on every correction.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `delivery-improvement`: optional independent observation, bounded diagnosis, lead decision and scheduling, result verification, lifecycle recovery and globally usable delivery.

### Modified Capabilities

- `orchestration-feedback-loop`: allow the designated observer to prepare bounded feedback, prevent derived observations from inflating votes, and add explicit evidence-based admission under the activated mode while preserving ordinary routing and evaluation boundaries.

## Impact

Extend the existing Rust session/control/observation and feedback owners, role resolution, orchestration configuration and installation lifecycle. Add the owned skill and improvement-role instructions; update `team-lead`, `board-workflow` and their existing operational guides. Reuse native tests and real installed consumer acceptance.

Integrate with the role resolver planned in `separate-lead-executor-contexts`; coordinate checkout allocation with `unify-reusable-worktree-pool`. Those changes remain separate owners with unfinished acceptance. No new task tracker, provider, generic agent framework, transcript warehouse or edits to protected OpenSpec workflows are proposed. This change contains planning artifacts only.
