# Proposal

## Why

Lead corrections and executor friction can expose avoidable work, but a persistent observer adds ongoing cost and lifecycle complexity. The user selected two short-lived native forks after accepted executor work: executor self-reflection followed by lead self-reflection, with decisions and implementation retained by the real lead.

The user also set the release boundary: one successful real sequential chain, from executor reflection through lead synthesis to delivery to the current live lead, is sufficient for harness release. The live lead decides whether any recommendation warrants ordinary follow-up. A mandatory comparative campaign, a required improvement implementation, or a quantified benefit threshold is not a release condition.

## What Changes

- Replace the unaccepted persistent-observer design and keep its withdrawn active code, role and skill selection out of delivery. Retained patches and private evidence are recovery inputs only.
- Add a default-on `self_reflection_enabled` permission in `global/orchestration.toml`. It gates new reflection model spending without activating a project: false stops new executor and lead stages across manual, automatic, continuation and internal dispatch while ordinary lead, executor and acceptance work remains available. Retained results, pending identities, usage and recovery are preserved. Ordinary acceptance remains successful while false, resolves the effective permission from an explicit source or the installed kit configuration without changing project pool provenance, and snapshots it in legacy-compatible metadata and its receipt. Default-true bindings omit the field; false remains explicit. The existing ignored-event result keeps such closures ineligible after re-enable, which does not sweep or replay them.
- Deliver `executor-self-reflection` and `lead-self-reflection` as separate skills with role-specific responsibilities and shared launch/delivery mechanics. Each fork analyzes the exact relevant source conversation under a bounded reflection mandate; it cannot resume the source task, edit product or guidance, create tasks or dispatch agents.
- Route the executor reflection through the configured executor profile, `ds` in the shipped kit. Route lead synthesis through the configured lead profile. Verify the effective binding and reject silent model, provider or billing substitution.
- While explicitly enabled, use the consuming board's durable accepted-task closure events. A closed card alone is insufficient: the lead's acceptance must bind the ordinary assignment, source sessions and accepted result. No trigger occurs on an initial executor report, intermediate correction, cancellation, arbitrary closure or elapsed time.
- Run executor reflection first. Retain its result, then make the corresponding lead reflection eligible and supply that executor result as input. The lead fork receives its own visible conversation, verifies both sides of corrections, and returns concrete recommendations or no finding to the current live lead.
- Require the synthesis to be consumed by the current live lead; queued, submitted, printed or retained output alone is not delivery. The live lead decides whether any recommendation warrants a standard executor assignment, deferral, rejection or no action. A no-finding result and a decision not to implement are valid outcomes.
- Mark ordinary work, reflection and improvement implementation in owned launch/task metadata. Reflection and improvement descendants cannot automatically trigger further reflection. Keep main work moving and at most one ordinary improvement implementation in flight within existing capacity. Reflection is not a routine release gate; a lead may hold an affected release only for a concrete incident risk, with a recorded reason and checkable release condition.
- Preserve visible titled conversations, exact role skills, finite cumulative cost records, interruption and recovery behavior, installation lifecycle, public/private boundaries and real consumption. Missing usage remains unknown and no savings claim is inferred without evidence.
- Accept release after one real sequential installed chain completes, the current live lead receives and acts on the synthesis disposition, applicable current regression checks pass, and global installation is verified. Optional later evaluation may inform future decisions; it is neither a release prerequisite nor a claim of measured savings.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `delivery-improvement`: accepted-task self-reflection, role-specific synthesis, bounded lead decisions and implementation, nonrecursive lifecycle and verified shared delivery.

### Modified Capabilities

- `orchestration-feedback-loop`: derived reflection evidence, lead-owned task creation and explicit verified-improvement admission without synthetic votes or recursive improvement work.

## Impact

Reuse the delivered role resolver, native fork/control/terminal/receipt owners, shared admission, board feedback, existing beads event journal and installation lifecycle. Extend maintained Rust owners only for remaining gaps; no new provider, task tracker, generic scheduler, transcript warehouse or model observer. Two skills replace the proposed observer skill and role. Reconcile team-lead and board-workflow guidance and existing operational docs.

The retained real accepted task completed executor reflection, lead synthesis with that executor result, and verified consumption and disposition by its current live lead. Earlier failed work and its costs remain preserved. The two canonical skills contain the same role instruction bodies exercised by that chain. Global delivery and the installed check outside the checkout passed, with both role skills resolved and unrelated configuration preserved. This operational result establishes neither comparative benefit nor savings.
