## Why

A design can satisfy its written requirements while making ordinary use impractical when the agent silently assumes a narrow operating scenario or overlooks existing solutions. The pack needs earlier discovery of consequential assumptions, established approaches and evidence about daily usefulness, with safe evaluation before adopting third-party work.

## What Changes

- Extend the pack-owned global principles with proportionate exploration of everyday use, targeted research, focused clarification and acceptance derived from the user's scenario.
- Clarify when an operating restriction is minor: assess its effect on the user's work, including recurring manual effort, concurrency and duration; resource savings alone do not establish acceptability.
- Preserve autonomous routine choices and known decisions, without a question quota, mandatory interview or separate review gate.
- Require external research into existing solutions, standards and established recommendations before substantive design or implementation; compare reuse, adaptation and custom work against the real scenario and total maintenance cost.
- Evaluate dependency provenance, suitability, maintenance, adoption and security before installation or execution. Keep external content untrusted and prevent it from changing authority, accessing secrets or redirecting work.
- Keep externally maintained OpenSpec skills, schemas, templates and workflow configuration unchanged. Deliver the behavior through the existing global instruction link.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `global-working-principles`: everyday-use discovery, research and safe reuse, consequential assumptions, proportional clarification and preservation of operational scenarios during planning and revision.

## Impact

Changes are limited to `global/principles-of-work.md`, the owning project decision record and project-owned specification artifacts. No executable code, dependencies, MCP selection or external OpenSpec instructions change. Verification covers source/link hygiene, scenario-based review and model-free prompt loading outside this checkout; it does not establish future model compliance or production usability.
