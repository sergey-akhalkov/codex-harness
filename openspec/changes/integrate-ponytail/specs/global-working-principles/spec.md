# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Global initial-context loading

After activation, the full shared portable core, selected Ponytail guidance and the applicable responsibility role SHALL be available before dependent work in the initial Codex context of new managed sessions across repositories using the configured Codex home. Role-owned orchestration obligations SHALL be loaded for the lead and role-owned execution obligations for executors, without omitting common safeguards. Activation SHALL use documented native instruction discovery and trusted native Ponytail hooks, preserve the ordinary instruction hierarchy, and distinguish pending trust from active guidance. Harness-owned instructions SHALL contain supplements rather than an equivalent copy of the upstream rules. Role composition SHALL follow `agent-role-contexts`; a lead role SHALL NOT itself activate delegation or require dispatch for a small direct task.

#### Scenario: Codex starts outside the harness repository
- **WHEN** a new main session starts in a separate repository with the activated Codex home
- **THEN** its initial context contains the shared core, Ponytail guidance and lead role without requiring a skill invocation or a manual file read

#### Scenario: Project guidance is also present
- **WHEN** the target repository has its own AGENTS.md
- **THEN** the shared core, Ponytail guidance, applicable role and project instructions are discoverable under Codex's normal precedence, without altering the project's source

#### Scenario: A new session delegates work
- **WHEN** a newly started session with the updated global source dispatches an executor
- **THEN** the shared core, Ponytail guidance and executor role are available in the delegated session's initial context without copying common policy into the role definition, requiring a manual source read or carrying the kit-owned lead-only instructions

### Requirement: Portable source and bounded activation

The reusable harness supplements SHALL be portable and independent of a particular machine path, shell or project. Ponytail-owned guidance SHALL be supplied by the declared external package, with its accepted revision and activation conditions explicit; a reference alone SHALL NOT establish that the guidance was loaded. Host activation SHALL preserve existing instructions and unrelated configuration, authentication, and session state. Harness-owned source SHALL remain in this repository, with the relationship to the active global file documented. Upstream instruction bodies SHALL remain in their original package and SHALL NOT be copied into the global file as a second maintained policy.

#### Scenario: An existing global instruction file or override is discovered
- **WHEN** activation encounters an existing instruction source
- **THEN** it preserves that source and resolves composition before replacing or obscuring any existing rules

### Requirement: Traceable adaptation

Supporting documentation SHALL identify the relevant harness and Ponytail source revisions, account for every original principle through a retained, adapted, consolidated or upstream-owned disposition, and record the user's resolution of substantive tensions. Detailed comparison and verification notes SHALL remain outside the always-loaded principles in the existing owning documentation. An upstream-owned disposition SHALL identify the original rule location, retained harness supplement where needed, and evidence of actual consumption without reproducing the upstream body.

#### Scenario: A maintainer asks why the fresh-review rule differs
- **WHEN** the maintainer reads the adaptation record
- **THEN** it explains the original rule, the concrete trade-off, the risk-based replacement, and the user's confirmation

#### Scenario: An equivalent local rule is removed
- **WHEN** an instruction is classified as covered by Ponytail
- **THEN** its disposition links the selected upstream rule and applicable consumption evidence, preserves any additional harness obligation, and does not treat similar wording as proof of equivalence

### Requirement: Bounded principles instruction floor

The common portable principles document SHALL remain at most 24 KiB. The shared core, selected upstream guidance and separately owned role guidance together SHALL preserve every normative rule of this specification, including session-lifecycle economy guidance and the current managed-tool selection. Compression and partitioning SHALL NOT drop, weaken or redefine a requirement, and references to retired capabilities SHALL be removed. The document SHALL remain the single authoritative home of harness-owned shared principles; Ponytail SHALL own the rules assigned to it, and each role obligation SHALL have one declared owner. Required instruction load SHALL be measured across composed surfaces under `agent-role-contexts`, including upstream hook context and invoked skills, so extracting or outsourcing text cannot conceal growth. Existing size limits SHALL NOT be raised merely to accommodate the integration.

#### Scenario: Size and content after compression
- **WHEN** the common document and composed role guidance are measured and mapped against this specification
- **THEN** the common document is at most 24 KiB, every normative obligation is retained in its applicable owner, and the complete required load satisfies the role-context and existing concise-guidance contracts

#### Scenario: Retired capability reference
- **WHEN** a capability leaves the managed selection
- **THEN** its applicable instruction owner stops naming it as a live selection without losing the general rule it illustrated

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ponytail and harness have distinct instruction ownership

Equivalent behavior SHALL have one instruction owner. Rules and workflows covered by the selected Ponytail package SHALL be consumed from upstream; harness-authored instructions SHALL retain only additional obligations and integration guidance. Consolidation SHALL preserve acceptance, authority, evidence and protected external workflows across every affected instruction surface.

#### Scenario: A rule is fully covered upstream
- **WHEN** semantic comparison and actual loading establish equivalent Ponytail coverage
- **THEN** the harness removes its equivalent instructions from common, developer, role, owned skill and generated guidance surfaces where present, without introducing a copied fallback or another equivalent skill

#### Scenario: Coverage is partial
- **WHEN** Ponytail covers general reuse or testing guidance but the harness requires additional research, ownership, recovery or real-consumer acceptance
- **THEN** the harness retains those additional obligations as a supplement and does not weaken them to the upstream minimum

#### Scenario: Protected workflow or controlling instruction differs
- **WHEN** an upstream instruction conflicts with a protected OpenSpec workflow or a controlling task constraint
- **THEN** the controlling instruction remains effective, the specific conflict is reported, and integration does not edit protected workflows or use Ponytail to bypass their boundary

### Requirement: Upstream upgrades retain instruction coverage

A Ponytail revision change SHALL reassess affected rule ownership and verify retained obligations before acceptance. Coverage evidence SHALL use the candidate's actual source and installed context. A removed or changed upstream rule SHALL NOT silently erase a harness requirement, and passing text-size or source-membership checks SHALL NOT substitute for semantic and behavioral evidence.

#### Scenario: Upstream weakens a relied-on rule
- **WHEN** a proposed Ponytail update removes or changes behavior delegated to it
- **THEN** the update remains unaccepted until coverage is restored within the agreed ownership boundary or the user explicitly changes the requirement, with the prior accepted selection recoverable

#### Scenario: A workflow is renamed upstream
- **WHEN** the selected revision changes an upstream skill or its resources
- **THEN** affected routes and consumption checks are reconciled without creating an obsolete local workflow copy
