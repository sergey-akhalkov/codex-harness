## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Model and context efficiency within authorized routing

Optimization SHALL evaluate lead reasoning effort, unnecessary context, redundant preparation, waiting, worker execution and total delegation cost. Suitable substantial text/code work SHALL prefer Z.AI; visual and suitable routine work SHALL prefer Grok; GPT SHALL retain consequential reasoning and justified reserves. All OpenAI assignments SHALL remain in the Astra family without hidden provider, billing or Fast-mode changes. Reasoning effort SHALL be selected at supported task or turn boundaries according to uncertainty and risk, without restarting ongoing work solely to change effort. Changed effort defaults SHALL require comparable accepted-result evidence; token savings SHALL NOT justify a quality regression. Lost child results SHALL be distinguished from model/auth/quota failure and recovered through their owning mechanism before completed work is duplicated.

#### Scenario: A child ends without a final artifact
- **WHEN** evidence shows partial work without a model/auth/quota rejection
- **THEN** its visible state and delivery mechanism are reconciled without inferring provider unavailability or automatically transferring the work to GPT

#### Scenario: A lower effort candidate saves tokens but misses a defect
- **WHEN** that candidate fails unchanged acceptance
- **THEN** it does not become the global default

#### Scenario: Effort is selected without a fixed agent profile
- **WHEN** an assignment can use a supported lower effort without changing its required outcome
- **THEN** the lead selects that model/effort combination directly, the effective values are visible in its conversation pane, and no separate agent definition is required

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Quota-aware pacing across account windows

Dispatch SHALL consider fresh available account-limit observations, their scope, reset times and observed use, prioritizing conservation of GPT for leadership and hard decisions. Account state SHALL be shared by the controller's tasks on that account while task work and authorization remain separate. Missing or stale telemetry SHALL remain unknown, not zero or unlimited. Local request logs SHALL NOT be treated as an authoritative subscription remainder. No model call SHALL be made solely to estimate remaining quota. Pacing SHALL adjust new work allocation and supported concurrency without abandoning an active healthy executor, dropping accepted work or adding purchases.

#### Scenario: GPT use is consuming the available window rapidly
- **WHEN** comparable current observations show GPT capacity depleting faster than the remaining window and capable alternatives are available
- **THEN** new suitable execution work is shifted toward Z.AI and Grok while preserving capacity for lead decisions and acceptance

#### Scenario: Another project consumes the same account
- **WHEN** a limit observation includes consumption outside the current task
- **THEN** dispatch uses the account state and reporting does not claim all consumption belongs to this task

#### Scenario: A provider exposes no reliable remaining-quota interface
- **WHEN** its remainder cannot be read reliably
- **THEN** the controller uses observed availability and failures, labels the remainder unknown and avoids speculative model probes

### Requirement: Verified benefit without deadline-driven takeover

The accepted comparison SHALL use a bounded, predeclared set of representative complete tasks and unchanged quality checks. It SHALL include lead preparation, coordination, all provider work, waiting, correction and integration. Acceptance SHALL demonstrate reduced attributable GPT use and no material total-delivery-time regression beyond predeclared measurement tolerance, with unchanged correctness. Tests SHALL also show that a healthy slow worker is not raced, pressured or replaced to improve the reported timing. Inconclusive attribution SHALL be reported explicitly; unsupported weekly savings or continuous-throughput guarantees SHALL NOT be claimed. Failure to demonstrate the agreed benefit SHALL leave the benefit tasks open rather than narrowing acceptance.

#### Scenario: Delegation saves GPT use but adds repeated rework
- **WHEN** a matched scenario exceeds the declared delivery-time tolerance or fails a quality check
- **THEN** routing, assignment or verification is corrected and the affected comparison remains unfinished

#### Scenario: Comparable evidence supports the selected policy
- **WHEN** the defined tasks pass unchanged checks with lower attributable GPT use and acceptable elapsed time
- **THEN** the selected policy records the evidence and its limits without converting token savings into an invented weekly allowance percentage
