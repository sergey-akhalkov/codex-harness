# Sequential improvement records

Beads remains the sole owner of task, hypothesis and decision status. Native
`codex-harness feedback hypothesis-*` commands retain investigation, trial,
implementation, decision and unadopted-reconcile references on the existing
cards; `removal-*` verbs own the user's scoped removal decisions. Read the
installed command help for request fields; do not maintain another hypothesis
journal. The `codex-harness improve start|status|select|stop|resume` cursor
records recovery position only and references these owners; phases that need
the separate comparison-execution or frozen-runtime owners stay pending until
an actual effect records them. `status` reports the phase cursor, gates,
attempts, the directed-measurement state and the decision-boundary receipts
with no model call. Operation and method selection are in the
[self-improvement-loop skill](../../self-improvement-loop/SKILL.md); command
details are in the
[native guide](../../../../docs/rust-native.md#improvement-controller).

Search open, closed and deferred hypotheses before admission. A repeated trial
uses the same hypothesis card. Reconsider an earlier same-condition rejection
only with a new recorded basis linked to the earlier conclusion. Candidate A
and workload B have a nonblocking relationship: finishing A's investigation
does not require a benefit verdict for B. This hypothesis path does not
manufacture incubator votes or change the ordinary feedback-promotion policy.

Each hypothesis - even a one-line instruction change - references its own
complete OpenSpec change from targeted initial measurement through
formulation, experiment design, implementation, remeasurement and the
decision; an evaluation workload keeps its own card and change separate from
the candidate's. The hypothesis must state the declared measurement scope
before any directed baseline measurement, and the change's artifacts and
acceptance must qualify before candidate edits. Retain attributable
observations separately from inferences and predictions. A trial references
exact source, specification, runtime and policy identities, both arms,
acceptance results, metric coverage and useful artifacts. The candidate
branch/worktree binds to the hypothesis card and the exact committed base;
prepared variants are immutable identities, and a completed arm is reused
only while its planning inputs and qualification still validate. Private
evidence stays in the owning local project; public board output contains no
private deployment details or raw traces.

Use the existing benefit-gate publication for adopt, reject or inconclusive
results. A recorded decision is not proof that acceptance ran. Missing,
contradictory or expired evidence cannot authorize adoption. Publish retries
idempotently without erasing failed attempts. Keep integration, experimental
activation and live publication references distinct from the benefit result.
An adopted decision integrates only the exact supported candidate; a rejection
or inconclusive result leaves the accepted baseline unchanged and retains the
candidate, evidence and reason.

No change, reuse, simplification and subtraction are considered before an
addition, and size alone is no efficiency evidence. A subtraction or
simplification proposal is admitted only with a coverage, lost-use,
consumption and restoration basis; low usage is a lead for investigation,
never a finding of uselessness or an automatic deletion. Removal proposals and
explicit user decisions use the same card via `feedback removal-propose`,
`removal-decide` and `removal-check`. Bind consent to the current reviewed
proposal, source, preview, lost scenarios and named stages. Pending, refused,
withdrawn or changed scope cannot inherit old consent; benefit alone cannot
authorize disabling, consolidation or deletion; and a refusal is not re-asked
without a new evidential basis. Prepare evidence, consumer/installation
impact, alternatives, retained checks and restoration before asking.

After the A-on-B decision, retain the independently checked B solutions and
select one exact B candidate for B-on-C. An A rejection keeps H unchanged;
another valid B solution is a different candidate. A declared successor starts
only after a completed decision with retained lineage; B-on-C is optional, and
absolute durations of different B and C tasks are not compared as a speed
trend. Preserve rejected experiment specifications without synchronizing their
unadopted requirements. An unadopted decision reconciles its own change through
the supported retain/archive path; an experiment outcome does not close
unfinished required OpenSpec work.
