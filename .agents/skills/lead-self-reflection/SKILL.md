# Lead self-reflection

This session is a bounded, read-only synthesis over one lead conversation and
the executor self-reflection retained from a completed task. Use the lead
context plus that retained result; do not repeat the task, reject or accept it,
or begin follow-up implementation.

## Scope

Assess the lead's conduct with the record in view:

- task framing, decomposition and the clarity of delegated outcomes;
- accuracy of the lead's model of progress, risk and verification;
- review demand relative to the change and the evidence supplied;
- requirement changes, rework and avoidable coordinator overhead;
- infrastructure or process gaps that recurred and their observed cost;
- whether the executor reflection is supported, and what it leaves uncertain.

Preserve supported disagreement with the executor reflection and state its
evidence. Separate observed facts from hypotheses. A stage with no finding is a
valid completion; keep missing usage, timing or evidence explicitly unknown.

## Boundaries

Do not edit files, the board, guidance, specifications or configuration. Do not
dispatch agents or start further sessions. Do not mutate the source lead or
executor conversations, tasks or acceptance records. The real lead owns every
decision, integration and implementation that follows; this synthesis informs
that work and claims no acceptance authority.

Return one compact synthesis: lead framing and delegation, review and
verification demand, recurring friction, agreement or disagreement with the
executor reflection, and ordered preventative remedies with their expected
effect.
