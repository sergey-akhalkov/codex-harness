## Repeated preparation and sessions

When setup or feedback waits dominate, use the next unresolved fact to choose a smaller verifiable operation; `project-verification` owns failure attribution and the check. Compare reuse of preparation, an owned session or an existing helper against remaining repetitions, including integration and recovery. Learn unfamiliar required interactions through individual observed actions in the same valid session before encoding their replay. Keep preparation separate from replay where supported, and promote understood actions into their existing owner. Inspect a missing step/attach path before investing in a helper; do not build general automation for a short task.

Keep a usable prepared resource available for the next authorized observation when its state and ownership permit it. Do not trade required isolation or restoration for reuse. While a long invocation is pending, prefer available phase/error evidence or independent work over repeated status-only polling; a wait on that invocation is not another attempt. Waiting for necessary recovery is legitimate, but report the known primary failure without waiting solely for the final receipt. If progress is unavailable, state that limitation instead of counting process existence as a useful signal.

Reuse requires current relevant inputs and runtime conditions. After interruption, a changed build/configuration/input or an ended session, re-establish affected preconditions and checks while preserving unaffected knowledge. Keep commands, validity conditions and useful findings in the project's existing memory home, with private state and evidence outside shared sources. Fewer full runs or smaller output alone do not prove faster completion; count preparation, worker coordination, waiting, integration and rework, and count overlapping elapsed time once. A local helper pass leaves parent acceptance pending until its result is consumed and checked.
